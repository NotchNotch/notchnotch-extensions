# Code of conduct

NotchNotch is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, languages
and levels of experience. This applies to everyone in its repositories, issues,
pull requests, reviews and Discussions.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people are new to something.
- Critique work, not people. In review, say what to change and why.
- Write so that someone reading in their second language can follow you.
- Respect a "no", whether it's to a feature, an extension in the Store or a
  conversation.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after a maintainer has decided.
- Using an extension, issue or review to spam, advertise or mislead.

## Reports

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, AmirHossein Sadeghi, by email at
the address on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/sadeqi-ah), or with
GitHub's **Report content** on the comment. Reports are read within a few days
and kept confidential.

## What happens

Depending on what happened, a maintainer may ask for a change, hide or edit
comments, close a pull request or issue, remove an extension from the Store, or
block someone from the project. Decisions are explained to the person, except
where that would put someone else at risk.

This code is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
