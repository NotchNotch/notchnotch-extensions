# Hacker News

The top stories on Hacker News, a click away.

## How it works

- Hover over the notch to see the top three stories. Click one to open it, or the bubble for its comments.
- Switch between Top, Best, New, Show HN and Ask HN in the extension's settings, where the top ten are listed.
- Refreshed every 10 minutes.

## Privacy

Reaches only `hacker-news.firebaseio.com`, Hacker News's official API.
