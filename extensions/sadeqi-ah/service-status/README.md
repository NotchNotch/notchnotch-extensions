# Service Status

Know right away when GitHub, Cloudflare or another service you rely on has an incident.

## How it works

- Choose services in the extension's settings: GitHub, Cloudflare, OpenAI, Claude, Discord, npm, Vercel, Atlassian and Dropbox.
- When one has an incident, it appears beside the camera and is announced below the notch.
- Click a service to open its status page. Checked every 5 minutes.

## Privacy

Reaches only the status pages of the services listed above, through their public Statuspage API.
