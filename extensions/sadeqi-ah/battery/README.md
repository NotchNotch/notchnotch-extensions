# Battery

Your battery in the notch: a ring while it charges, and a heads-up when it runs low.

## How it works

- While your Mac charges, the notch shows a green ring and the percentage. Plugging in shows "Charging" for a moment.
- When the battery drops below the level you choose (20% by default) and the Mac isn't plugged in, the ring turns red and the notch lets you know once.
- The rest of the time Battery stays out of the way: hover over the notch to see it.
- Time to full or to empty appears once macOS has an estimate, and Low Power Mode shows as a badge.
- Choose the low level, or turn off the charging ring, under Setup.

NotchNotch reads the battery itself, so Battery asks for nothing and makes no network requests. Needs NotchNotch 0.11 or later. Built with NotchNotch SDK 0.11.
