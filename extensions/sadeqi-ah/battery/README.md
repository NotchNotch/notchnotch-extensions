# Battery

Your battery in the notch: a heads-up when it starts charging or runs low.

## How it works

- Plugging in shows "Charging" and the percentage for a moment.
- When the battery drops below the level you choose (20% by default) and the Mac isn't plugged in, the notch lets you know once.
- Battery never stays beside the camera: hover over the notch to see it.
- Time to full or to empty appears once macOS has an estimate, and Low Power Mode shows as a badge.
- Choose the low level, or turn off the charging heads-up, under Setup.

NotchNotch reads the battery itself, so Battery asks for nothing and makes no network requests. Needs NotchNotch 0.11 or later. Built with NotchNotch SDK 0.11.
