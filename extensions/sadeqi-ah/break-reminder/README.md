# Break Reminder

A gentle nudge to stand up and rest your eyes at regular intervals.

## How it works

- Choose how often in the extension's settings: every 20 minutes to every 90.
- When it's time, a prompt suggests a short break. Choose **Done** or **In 10 Minutes**.
- If your Mac slept or you were away for more than 15 minutes, the timer starts over instead of reminding you about a break you already took.

## Privacy

Works offline.
