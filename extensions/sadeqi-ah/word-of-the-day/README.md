# Word of the Day

A new English word every day, with its meaning and an example.

## How it works

- Hover over the notch to see today's word, how to say it, what it means and how it's used.
- A new word is announced each day.
- **More on Wiktionary** opens its full entry.

## Privacy

Definitions from [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org) via the [Free Dictionary API](https://dictionaryapi.dev), under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Reaches only `api.dictionaryapi.dev`.
