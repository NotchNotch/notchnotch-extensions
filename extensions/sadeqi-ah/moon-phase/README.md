# Moon Phase

Tonight's moon, how much of it is lit, and when the next full moon rises.

## How it works

- Hover over the notch to see the phase, how much of the moon is lit, and the next full and new moons.
- On the day of a full moon, the notch lets you know.

## Privacy

Works offline: calculated on your Mac from the average length of a lunar cycle, so dates can be off by a few hours.
