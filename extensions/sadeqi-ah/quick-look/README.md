# Quick Look

Drop files on the notch to preview them in Quick Look.

## How it works

- Drag files or folders to the notch and let go on **Quick Look**. They open in the Quick Look panel; use the arrow keys to move between them.
- Quick Look keeps nothing: once the panel has the files, it forgets them.
- It shows only while you drag something it takes; it has no activity in Home.

Quick Look asks for nothing and makes no network requests. It never sees where your files are, only their names. Needs NotchNotch 0.14 or later. Built with NotchNotch SDK 0.14.
