# Weather

Current conditions, the feels-like temperature and today's high and low for your city.

## How it works

- Set your city and units in **Setup**.
- The temperature sits beside the camera; turn off **Show Beside the Notch** to see it only when you hover.
- Updates every 15 minutes.

## Privacy

Weather data from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) (CC BY 4.0). Reaches `api.open-meteo.com` and `geocoding-api.open-meteo.com` to look up your city and its weather.
