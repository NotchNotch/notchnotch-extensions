# Calendar Changelog

## [1.0.0]

- Now in the Store. It used to come with NotchNotch; if you had it, NotchNotch installs this version for you and keeps your chosen calendars.
- NotchNotch reads your calendars itself, with the `calendar` capability, instead of a helper program. It asks once whether Calendar may read them.
- Needs NotchNotch 0.9 or later.
